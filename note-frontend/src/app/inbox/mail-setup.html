Connect your Gmail

MyNotion reads your Primary inbox (no ads, newsletters or spam) and sends with your account. It uses an app password, a separate password just for MyNotion. It's kept in your system keyring, not in MyNotion's files.

  1. Turn on 2-step verification for your Google account, if it isn't on yet.
  2. Make an app password at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords (any name, e.g. "MyNotion").
  3. Paste it here with your address.
